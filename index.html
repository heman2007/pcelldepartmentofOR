About the Placement Cell

The Placement Cell of the Department of Operational Research coordinates campus recruitment for the department's students. It works with employers to schedule pre-placement talks, tests and interviews, and it helps students prepare for every stage, from resumes to technical and case rounds.

Students of the department are trained to turn business questions into models: they formulate the problem, choose the method, and communicate the result to people who have to act on it.

  • Optimisation and simulation

    Linear and integer programming, queueing, inventory and supply-chain models.

  • Statistics and machine learning

    Regression, forecasting, classification and the design of experiments.

  • Analytics tools

    Python, R, SQL and spreadsheet modelling applied to real business data.

  • Domains

    Banking and risk, consulting, insurance, retail analytics, shipping and logistics.

    Recruit from the department

    Hiring through the Placement Cell takes four steps. The cell handles scheduling, rooms, links and student communication.

    1. Share your requirement

      Send the job description, CTC and preferred dates to the Placement Cell.

    2. Pre-placement talk

      Present the role and the team to students on campus.

    3. Selection rounds

      Run tests and technical or HR interviews on campus with the cell's support.

    4. Offers and joining

      Release offers; the cell coordinates acceptances and joining dates.
